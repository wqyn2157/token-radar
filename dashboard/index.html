How this ranking works

Every protocol on this board records no token in the snapshot — no market cap, no CoinGecko id, no ticker — and is not a wrapped, staked or bridged product of a token that already trades, not an exchange, and not a versioned pool of another protocol. The board covers the whole watchlist the snapshot names and keeps going below its TVL floor under the same rule.

The implied value is a claim built only from the snapshot's own figures. Protocols that already trade say what a dollar of TVL is worth in their category: the median market-cap-to-TVL multiple of a category's tokenised protocols, shrunk toward the all-market median when a category has few peers, and clamped between 0.03x and 2.50x. A protocol's base value is its TVL times its category's multiple, then three adjustments apply: momentum (its 7-day TVL change, clamped to −50%…+100%, weighted 0.6), maturity (how long DefiLlama has tracked it with no token yet, 0.80x–1.15x over three years), and reach (how many chains it runs on, up to 1.25x). The score is that implied value on a log scale, 0–100. Every row's detail view shows each factor, so any line of this board can be recomputed by hand.

Two honest limits. This says what a launch would be worth if the protocol's record stayed as it is; it does not predict that a token will be issued, or when. And "records no token" is DefiLlama's record, not proof that none exists.

Implied values are this radar's own model output, not a promise of a token, a price, or an airdrop. Farm plans are starting points for your own research; every step carries real cost and risk, and no plan here is financial advice.
